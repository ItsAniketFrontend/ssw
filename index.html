Ayurveda, A Way of Living Well

Ayurveda looks at wellbeing as a whole. It considers the way your body, mind, daily routines and surroundings come together, and encourages you to listen to what your body needs. By bringing greater balance into everyday life, Ayurveda offers a thoughtful approach to caring for yourself. At Sri Sri Wellbeing, this wisdom comes together with modern wellness practices to create personalized experiences that help you slow down, reconnect with yourself and make space for your wellbeing.

Ask Us Directly
What is Ayurveda and how does it approach wellbeing?

Ayurveda takes a holistic view of wellbeing, considering the individual as a whole. It looks at lifestyle, routines, nourishment and individual needs to encourage greater balance in everyday life.

Which wellness experience is right for me?

Each person’s needs are different. Our practitioners can help you understand the therapies and wellness experiences that may be appropriate for you based on your individual needs and preferences.

What can I expect during my first visit?

Your experience begins with an understanding of your needs. Depending on the service you choose, a practitioner may discuss your lifestyle and concerns before recommending a suitable wellness approach.

Are the therapies personalised?

Yes. We believe wellbeing is personal. Our approach takes into consideration your individual needs, preferences and wellness goals when guiding you towards suitable experiences.

Do I need to prepare anything before my appointment?

Preparation can vary depending on the therapy or wellness programme you choose. Our team will guide you on anything you need to know before your visit.

How do I choose between Ayurveda, Panchakarma, Osteopathy and Relaxation Therapies?

The right experience depends on what you are looking for. Our team can help you explore the different options and understand which may be best suited to your needs.

Can I speak to someone before booking?

Absolutely. You can connect with our team to understand our therapies, ask questions and find an experience that feels right for you.
